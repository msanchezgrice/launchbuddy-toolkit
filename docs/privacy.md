# Public and private boundaries

This repository intentionally includes public instructions, portable skills, synthetic examples and endpoint documentation only. It excludes the private applications, account and billing implementation, provider credentials, operational access, customer data, private reports and internal Swarm submission records.

Public URL audits are bounded observations of public sites. They do not prove authenticated signup success, inbox receipt, customer demand or application security. Sharing a report is a separate decision. Keep assessment claims and authentication tokens out of exports, issue reports and screenshots.

Do not post security issues with exploitable or private details in a public issue. Use [LaunchBuddy contact](https://launchbuddy.com/contact). See the [service privacy policy](https://launchbuddy.com/privacy) and [terms](https://launchbuddy.com/terms).

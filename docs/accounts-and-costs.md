# Accounts, costs and authority

- Anonymous public-site assessment and resource discovery are free. LaunchBuddy's anonymous launch report has a shareable URL. AgentReady reports are private by default; optional unlisted reports expire after 30 days.
- A free connected identity owns saved project context, checklist progress and history. Preserve the original assessment through the private expiring claim rather than scanning again.
- `get_access(projectId)` is authoritative for the connected identity, project, trial eligibility and paid capabilities. Never send an existing customer to a second checkout because a client is disconnected or a different project was selected.
- `preview_recommended_asset` creates no provider spend. `create_recommended_asset` is a non-charging, resumable review intent. Provider execution begins only after the exact workspace card is approved and the server permits it.
- The eligible one-asset agent trial is separate from the managed workspace's free first week. Neither is a general credit balance or a paid Stripe entitlement. Read the exact live offer rather than inventing a credit grant.
- The managed workspace's advertised offer is $49/month after its free first week. Final price, eligibility and scope come from the live offer and account state. Installing a skill or connecting never authorizes payment.
- Asset approval does not authorize publishing. The public MCP provides no publish tool. Keep private reports and artifacts private until the person explicitly shares or publishes them.

The commercial strategy is to help someone complete a real task, then offer repeat hosted work when useful. A higher readiness score, an installed skill or a catalogue entry is not a purchase outcome.

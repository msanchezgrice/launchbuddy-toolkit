# Launch distribution

Use `search_launch_resources` with `source: swarm` and a task-specific query. A large catalogue is discovery material, not proof every channel accepts this product or supports automation.

For a short relevant list, inspect each canonical destination's current audience, eligibility, fees, account requirements, and submission policy. Preserve catalogue availability labels, including under-review and founder-only paths. Do not bypass anti-automation rules.

Prepare accurate product name, URL, category, tagline, description, screenshots, logo, and disclosure fields from the user's real product. Never invent usage, awards, quotes, or approval. Check existing listings and pending applications before submitting.

Follow the user's actual submission authorization. Record prepared, submitted, under review, accepted, published, and verified live separately, with receipts. Uncertain outcomes require checking the destination before a retry. Publication alone does not prove traffic or customers.

Reference: https://launchbuddy.com/sites and https://launchbuddy.com/blog/zero-to-first-100-users-a-distribution-playbook-for-vibe-coded-products.

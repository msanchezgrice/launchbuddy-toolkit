# Launch demo

Choose one audience and one job. Draft a short sequence: problem, action, visible result, next step. Use a demo account and synthetic data; exclude credentials and real customer information.

Inspect the product before claiming a feature works. Prepare a shot list, script, captions, screenshot/share-image brief, and review checklist. Screen recordings must show actual behavior. Label illustrative output; generated B-roll must not masquerade as a working interface.

Use available media tools within the user's authorization and budget. Keep proposed, generated, reviewed, approved, and published states separate. Review readability on a phone, audio/captions, accurate claims, the CTA, and export dimensions.

Return the prepared assets or source links, verification notes, and any needed review. A request for an audit does not authorize paid generation or publication.
